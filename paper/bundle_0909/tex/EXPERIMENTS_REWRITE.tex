\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}

E1 measures gaze-to-instance binding accuracy against angular
separation; E4 switches off each mechanism of
Sec.~\ref{sec:method} in bit-exact replay; E2/E3 run the full loop
on the robot, in and out of view of the target.

\subsection{Setup}
\label{sec:experimental_setup}

\textbf{Platform.} Pupil Core eye tracker; Unitree B2 quadruped with
an arm; iPhone 16 Pro Max for mapping; RTX 4090 workstation.
Speech: faster-whisper (word timestamps) and GPT-5 mini slot parsing.

\textbf{Maps.} One room, four independently arranged and mapped
layouts (78--144 photos; the two latest maps hold 259 and 64
instances, 13 and 11 named), including three identical tennis balls,
two identical white cups, and other everyday items a few centimeters
apart. No object carries a marker.

\textbf{Wearers.} Three; recalibration after every donning
(accuracy $0.8$--$1.1^\circ$). $\sigma=1^\circ$ throughout; E4
sweeps it.

\textbf{Protocol.} Pre-registered cards of 12--15 named targets,
each fixated 2--3\,s from a fixed station (\TBD{n-stations} stations,
$1.0$--$4.6$\,m). $\theta$ = minimum angular separation between the
target and any other named instance, computed from the median head
pose and the map, independent of outcome
($0.1$--$24^\circ$). Four recordings while walking laterally.

\textbf{Scoring.} Frozen resolver, offline replay; same-instance
outputs within $1.2$\,s merged, segments under $1.0$\,s dropped; the
longest-fixated instance in a target's slot is the prediction and
must match in sequence.

\textbf{Data hygiene.} All 330 static trials are reported except
three in which the wearer fixated the wrong ball (the system bound
the fixated ball). Two earlier recordings (30 trials) were dropped
for instrumentation faults before scoring (localization dead zone;
cart displaced $5.8$\,cm). Two pre-registered settings changed before
the final runs and are reported as run (table prior set to zero;
re-layout batch replaced by the four maps). The first author
performed 90\% of gaze trials; 39 robot trials, 20 with full logs.

\subsection{E1: Accuracy versus Angular Separation}
\label{sec:disambiguation_results}

Fig.~\ref{fig:theta}: $\theta\ge2.5^\circ$, 151/154 ($98.1\%$);
$1^\circ\le\theta<2.5^\circ$, 77/88 ($87.5\%$); $\theta<1^\circ$
with a clear sightline, 26/37 ($70.3\%$, CI $[54, 83]$); $\theta<1^\circ$
with the target physically occluded, 16/48 ($33.3\%$, CI $[22, 48]$;
chance $33\%$). Walking: 56/57 over four recordings. Second and
third wearers: 15/15 and 12/12.

Accuracy is governed by $\theta$ relative to the calibrated $\sigma$,
with the knee at $1$--$2.5^\circ$; below $\sigma$ the collapse to
chance occurs only when the target is occluded, so it is a
visibility limit of the scene, not a precision limit of the gaze
channel.

\subsection{E2, E3: End-to-End Retrieval}
\label{sec:end_to_end_results}

36/39 trials succeeded ($92\%$): gaze-resolved deictic references
28/30, category names 8/9, placements 2/2; both identical-cup
trials correct. Failures: wearer fixated a neighboring instance
(2), no binding above threshold, robot refused (1). Target-matched
pairs: 7/8 with the target in the robot's view, 7/8 starting outside
the room. Timing: accepted order to delivery $5.4$\,s median
($n{=}14$, IQR $5.3$--$7.4$); speech end to dispatch $2.2$\,s
(cached parse) / $5.0$\,s (cold LLM); emergency stop \TBD{sys-estop}.

Resolution is complete in the map before the robot moves; whether
the robot can see the target does not change the outcome.

\subsection{E4: Replay Ablations}
\label{sec:ablation_results}

Table~\ref{tab:ablations} (204-trial frozen subset; bins
$n=58/66/78$, walking $n=15$): removing directional uncertainty
(near-single ray) drops 166$\to$102 and walking 14$\to$3; removing
visible-surface voting (sphere) drops the sub-$\sigma$ bin 50$\to$15;
admitting places or all instances as candidates drops to 144 and 39
and walking to 2/15 and 2/15. Angular clustering ($+7$ sub-$\sigma$),
area normalization ($-1$ hit, fewer extra bindings) and the
interactability prior (hard bin and walking only) are refinements.

Fig.~\ref{fig:sigma}, $\sigma$ sweep: $1$--$2.5^\circ$ bin
29/42/58/58/47/26 of 66 and sub-$\sigma$ bin 23/34/50/38/27/15 of 78
for $\sigma=0.2/0.5/1/1.5/2.5/4^\circ$. The optimum is the calibrated
width.
